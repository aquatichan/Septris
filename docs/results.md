# Results

> This project is a prototype and is not medical advice.

Every number below is generated by `scripts/06_report.py` from `outputs/results/`. Rates are the mean of 5 admission orders; brackets are 95% intervals from 200 patient-level bootstrap replicates. Paired differences are computed inside each replicate.

## Headline

- **Hospital A:** with 3 reviews per 12-hour shift, Septris flagged 60 of 238 eligible sepsis cases (25.1% (21.2–31.2)) 6–12 hours before onset with its home model, and 15.5% (12.1–21.0) with the model trained at Hospital B. Random reviews under the same budget flagged 8.0% (5.3–12.6).
- **Hospital B:** with 3 reviews per 12-hour shift, Septris flagged 35 of 134 eligible sepsis cases (26.3% (19.8–33.8)) 6–12 hours before onset with its home model, and 23.7% (17.0–29.6) with the model trained at Hospital A. Random reviews under the same budget flagged 9.3% (4.2–13.7).

## Cohort and checks

- Eligible septic / uncertain onset / non-septic: A 1587 / 203 / 18546; B 919 / 223 / 18858. Uncertain-onset patients (203 + 223) arrive already within 6 h of onset: no 6-hour warning is possible for them.
- Cross-hospital rule dropped: EtCO2. Variables with very different coverage that the frozen rule kept: BaseExcess (A 62% vs B 3% of patients), HCO3 (A 97% vs B 2% of patients), Chloride (A 97% vs B 8% of patients), PTT (A 78% vs B 22% of patients).
- Null check: LightGBM trained on shuffled labels caught 9.0%, inside random review's interval 5.3%–12.6%: pass.
- Leakage, split, label, slot, alert-class and utility tests: see `tests/` (`python -m pytest -q tests`).
- Too-good check: highest internal hourly AUROC of a portable model is 0.804 (alarm threshold 0.95).
